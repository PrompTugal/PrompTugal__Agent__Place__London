The first strike, as TfL planned it

Normal service until about 10:00 on 26 Nov, then disruption. No service on the Circle or Piccadilly lines all day and none on the Waterloo & City line after midday. On 27 Nov services expected to start from about 07:00, disruption until about midday, back to normal by about 18:00. Elizabeth line, DLR, London Overground, buses and trams expected to run, possibly busier than usual. Line detail for the other seven strikes has not been published.

The dispute, in each side's words

TfL: TfL says it concerns revised proposals for a voluntary four-day week for train drivers; drivers can stay on five days at the same depot; same pay for 35 hours; the ASLEF union has accepted (TfL's claim, not checked with ASLEF).

RMT: The RMT says the plan, with a Trains Modernisation Framework, means more time driving, longer duties, short-notice shift changes and loss of protections whether drivers work four or five days.

We do not say who is right.
